# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

This repository contains the core project management guidance used by OctoAcme across the project lifecycle. The documents are intended to provide a shared source of truth for planning, execution, communication, and continuous improvement across cross-functional work.

## Project management process summary

OctoAcme follows a simple, repeatable lifecycle for project work: initiation, planning, execution, release, and retrospective. During initiation, teams confirm the business need, define stakeholders and success metrics, and produce a lightweight project one-pager. Planning then turns the approved initiative into a prioritized backlog with clear acceptance criteria, estimates, dependencies, and a timeline for milestones. Execution focuses on delivery through daily coordination, sprint planning, risk management, and visible progress tracking. Once work is ready, release and deployment steps ensure pre-release checks, smoke tests, stakeholder communication, and rollback readiness. Every milestone closes with a retrospective to capture what went well, what needs improvement, and which action items should be carried forward.

## Key workflow and practices

OctoAcme emphasizes customer value, clear ownership, and iterative delivery. Teams align around a problem statement, measurable outcomes, and stakeholder expectations before work begins. As work progresses, the project manager, product lead, and delivery team coordinate through standups, weekly syncs, and milestone reviews while tracking risks, blockers, and dependencies. The process also defines standard quality expectations, including unit and integration testing, smoke tests for critical flows, PR review discipline, and security scanning before release. Retrospectives help turn learning into continuous improvement actions with owners and due dates.

## Roles and responsibilities

The project docs define key roles that support the lifecycle. Project Managers coordinate delivery plans, schedule risk management, and maintain cross-team communication. Product Managers define the desired outcomes, success metrics, and backlog priorities. Developers build, test, and improve the solution. QA and testing partners validate quality and acceptance criteria. Stakeholders provide context, review decisions, and support business alignment. Clear role ownership helps teams stay aligned while minimizing drift and unplanned work.

These documents help the team maintain consistent delivery practices, align around shared operating principles, and provide a practical framework for managing work from idea to release. They are designed to reduce single-person dependency risk, improve project transparency, and support repeatable execution across OctoAcme projects.
