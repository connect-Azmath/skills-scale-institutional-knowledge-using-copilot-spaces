# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Technical Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Architects define the system design, technology standards, and integration approach. They ensure the solution is technically sound, scalable, and aligned with business goals.

### Responsibilities
- Define architecture principles, standards, and technical guardrails
- Review major design decisions and integration points
- Partner with developers and Engineering Lead on solution feasibility and trade-offs
- Align technical choices with maintainability, security, and long-term scalability
- Support risk identification and mitigation for complex technical dependencies

### Goals
- Maintain coherent system design across services and capabilities
- Reduce avoidable technical debt and architectural drift
- Enable delivery teams to build with confidence and consistency

### Typical Communication
- Architecture reviews and technical design discussions
- Cross-team dependency planning and platform decisions
- Working sessions with PM, Product Lead, and engineering leadership

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Developers to ensure implementation matches the target architecture
- Collaborates with Project Managers and Product Managers to assess feasibility, sequencing, and risk
- Provides technical context that informs Engineering Lead execution decisions

---

## Engineering Lead

### Role Summary
Engineering Leads coordinate technical delivery, sprint execution, and engineering trade-offs. They translate product priorities into actionable delivery plans while balancing quality, capacity, and dependencies.

### Responsibilities
- Lead engineering planning and delivery coordination for the team
- Own sprint-level execution health, capacity alignment, and risk escalation
- Partner with Product Managers and Project Managers on scope, sequencing, and dependencies
- Support developers in resolving technical blockers and decision-making
- Help maintain engineering quality, delivery predictability, and team alignment

### Goals
- Deliver work reliably and predictably within agreed scope and timeline
- Reduce delivery friction caused by unclear priorities or dependencies
- Keep technical execution aligned with product goals and team capacity

### Typical Communication
- Engineering planning, sprint reviews, and delivery standups
- Escalation updates with PM and Product Lead
- Technical trade-off discussions with architects and stakeholders

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works closely with Developers to clarify priorities and unblock execution
- Coordinates with Project Managers to manage risks, dependencies, and timelines
- Advises Product Managers on feasibility, sequencing, and technical trade-offs

---

## Release Manager / Delivery Lead

### Role Summary
Release Managers or Delivery Leads coordinate release readiness, deployment planning, rollback planning, and communications around customer-facing changes.

### Responsibilities
- Plan release windows, launch readiness, and deployment sequencing
- Coordinate validation across engineering, QA, and stakeholders before release
- Manage rollback plans and incident communication when issues arise
- Ensure release notes and stakeholder updates are prepared in advance
- Track release health and post-deployment verification

### Goals
- Minimize release risk and customer disruption
- Improve predictability and operational readiness for launches
- Ensure teams are aligned on release timing, dependencies, and ownership

### Typical Communication
- Release checklists, readiness reviews, and deployment approvals
- Stakeholder updates before and after launch
- Coordination with PM, QA, and engineering leadership

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Developers and QA Lead to confirm release criteria are met
- Reports status to Project Managers and Product Managers for stakeholder communication
- Coordinates handoff between product delivery and customer-facing or support teams

---

## Security & Compliance Lead

### Role Summary
Security and Compliance Leads review project risks, controls, and policy requirements to ensure work is delivered securely and in compliance with relevant standards.

### Responsibilities
- Identify security, privacy, and compliance requirements for the initiative
- Review architectures, workflows, and data handling for policy alignment
- Partner with engineering and stakeholders on controls, mitigation plans, and approvals
- Support security reviews, audit readiness, and incident response coordination
- Track open risks and remediation work throughout the project lifecycle

### Goals
- Reduce security and compliance risk without slowing delivery unnecessarily
- Ensure the solution meets required controls and governance expectations
- Build confidence with stakeholders, regulators, and support teams

### Typical Communication
- Security review meetings, compliance checkpoints, and risk assessments
- Escalation of critical concerns to PM, Product Lead, and sponsor stakeholders
- Coordination with engineering and operational teams on mitigations

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Technical Architect and Developers to validate designs and controls
- Collaborates with Project Managers to ensure risk mitigation is visible and tracked
- Supports Product Managers and stakeholders in understanding compliance trade-offs and impacts

---

## QA Lead

### Role Summary
QA Leads define quality standards, validation approaches, and release readiness criteria to ensure the solution meets acceptance and customer expectations.

### Responsibilities
- Define test strategy, quality gates, and acceptance validation criteria
- Coordinate test planning across unit, integration, smoke, and regression coverage
- Review defects, quality trends, and release confidence with the team
- Partner with Developers and Product Managers to validate business outcomes and readiness
- Ensure quality is considered early in planning and throughout delivery

### Goals
- Prevent issues from reaching production by validating early and often
- Increase confidence in the quality and usability of delivered work
- Align quality expectations across engineering and stakeholder groups

### Typical Communication
- Test planning, defect triage, and release readiness reviews
- Acceptance validation and quality metrics updates
- Coordination with PM, Product Lead, and release stakeholders

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works closely with Developers and Engineering Lead to prioritize quality issues and test coverage
- Reports readiness and risk signals to Project Managers and Product Managers
- Provides confidence checks that support release decisions and stakeholder communication

---

## Customer Success / Stakeholder Liaison

### Role Summary
Customer Success or Stakeholder Liaison roles translate stakeholder needs, customer feedback, and operational realities into project priorities and communication. They help ensure the team stays close to user impact and adoption outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Gather voice-of-customer feedback, stakeholder concerns, and adoption challenges
- Translate customer and stakeholder needs into priorities, requirements, or risks
- Support readiness communications and change management activities
- Monitor service impact and feedback after launch or rollout
- Facilitate handoff between delivery teams and support or customer-facing stakeholders

### Goals
- Improve customer and stakeholder satisfaction with delivery outcomes
- Keep product work connected to real-world impact and adoption
- Strengthen communication between delivery teams and business stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Stakeholder briefings, customer feedback reviews, and adoption check-ins
- Status updates and post-release communication with business stakeholders
- Coordination with Product Managers, PM, and support teams

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Brings customer and stakeholder context to Product Managers and Project Managers
- Helps prioritize issues, dependencies, and communication plans with the delivery team
- Works with Release Manager and QA Lead to align launch readiness with adoption needs

---

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- These roles are intended to complement the existing project management framework by clarifying ownership, decision-making, and cross-functional accountability.
